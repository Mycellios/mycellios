### Fixed

- Replies start sooner: the server no longer freezes for seconds while it saves an encrypted
  secret, which every push notification and connector token renewal did.
- Your message and a "Thinking it through…" line appear the moment you press Send, including
  in a new conversation, instead of after the server answers.
- A turn that has started reads as working instead of "Status not confirmed".
- Claude Code and Codex keep their conversation when a routine updates your notes or memory,
  instead of starting over and resending the whole history.
- The Usage page loads in about a second instead of tens of seconds.
- The usage panel opens on hover at once, shows OpenCode Go limits (5 hours, week and month)
  and no longer lists the same ChatGPT account twice.
- "Working in another conversation" only shows while your message is still waiting.

### Changed

- The conversation header is simpler: the Mico's name is the main chat tab, followed by your
  conversations, New conversation and a chevron for all conversations. Tasks stays in the rail
  and chat search opens with Ctrl/Cmd+F.
- Menus and popovers stand out from the page with a raised surface, a fine edge and a soft shadow.
- "Generate AI" is now "Earn credits", with a sprout that sways while your computer contributes and
  a new press animation; Credits show two decimals large, the rest small, and a gift icon for promotions.
- A working Mico shows three rising dots over its avatar and a light that moves across its line.
- Settings navigation is more compact on desktop.
