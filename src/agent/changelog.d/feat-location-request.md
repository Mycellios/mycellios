### Added

- A Mico can ask for your location when a request needs it, such as "restaurants near me".
  It shows a card in the chat and nothing is sent until you answer. Share location sends your
  current position once, rounded to about 100 m, in the web app, the installed phone app and
  the Android app; anywhere, including Desktop, you can type a place instead or choose not to
  share. The location reaches the Mico for that request only and is not saved in the chat.
